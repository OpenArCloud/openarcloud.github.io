Projects

Open AR Cloud projects develop, test, demonstrate, and advance open and interoperable spatial computing technologies. Explore what we are working on, and find your way to take part.

How Can I Contribute?

There are four ways individuals and organizations can take part in the Open AR Cloud ecosystem.

01

Contributor

For individual developers or organizations who want to contribute their knowledge, development work, research, documentation, testing, or other expertise to OARC projects.

02

Testbed

For organizations that want to provide a real-world environment where OARC technologies, standards, protocols, and implementations can be tested and demonstrated.

03

Testbed Service Provider

For organizations that provide technologies or services that can be integrated into and evaluated through OARC testbeds.

04

Implementer

For companies or organizations that implement OARC-supported standards and protocols in their products, platforms, services, or applications.

Our Projects

The projects Open AR Cloud is actively working on. Each project will have its own dedicated page.

OARC Project

SpatialDDS

A protocol specification for real-world spatial computing, defining how spatial data is discovered, distributed, and synchronized across devices and services.

Contribute →
OARC Project

OpenVPS

The open-source Visual Positioning Service reference implementation: camera-based localization that follows the OGC GeoPose standard and can be self-hosted without vendor lock-in.

Contribute →
OARC Project

Reality Modeling

Standardized descriptions of real-world environments, bringing together BIM, GIS, IoT, and 3D formats such as glTF, USD, and 3D Tiles.

OARC ProjectOGC Standard

GeoPose

The OGC standard for representing position and orientation in space, and the OSCP GeoPose Protocol that puts it to work in spatial computing services.

OARC Project

OSCP

The Open Spatial Computing Platform: an open, edge-first architecture for building spatially aware applications that connect the physical and digital worlds.

OARC Project

spARcl

spARcl is Open AR Cloud’s open-source WebXR spatial browser. It discovers the spatial services and content available at the user’s location and shows them in AR, from text, icons, and glTF 3D models to videos, point clouds, IoT sensor streams, and OGC points of interest.
