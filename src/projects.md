## How Can I Contribute?

<p class="section-intro">There are four ways individuals and organizations can take part in the Open AR Cloud ecosystem.</p>

<!-- Participation paths. Each card will link to the future "How Can I Contribute?" page.
     When that page exists, change the <div class="contribute-card"> to <a class="contribute-card" href="..."> . -->
<div class="contribute-grid">
    <div class="contribute-card">
        <span class="contribute-tag">01</span>
        <h3>Contributor</h3>
        <p>For individual developers or organizations who want to contribute their knowledge, development work, research, documentation, testing, or other expertise to OARC projects.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="contribute-card">
        <span class="contribute-tag">02</span>
        <h3>Testbed</h3>
        <p>For organizations that want to provide a real-world environment where OARC technologies, standards, protocols, and implementations can be tested and demonstrated.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="contribute-card">
        <span class="contribute-tag">03</span>
        <h3>Testbed Service Provider</h3>
        <p>For organizations that provide technologies or services that can be integrated into and evaluated through OARC testbeds.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="contribute-card">
        <span class="contribute-tag">04</span>
        <h3>Implementer</h3>
        <p>For companies or organizations that implement OARC-supported standards and protocols in their products, platforms, services, or applications.</p>
    </div>
</div>
